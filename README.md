# JIMZ AI Studio

Build a premium, modern, international website for a technology and creative company called JIMZ AI.

BRAND:

Company name: JIMZ AI

Tagline: AI CREATIVE & DIGITAL SOLUTIONS

JIMZ AI is an AI-powered creative and digital solutions company that helps businesses use artificial intelligence to create content, improve digital experiences, automate workflows and solve business problems.

The initial flagship service is AI-generated content and AI video production, but the website must be architected so the company can expand into broader AI services in the future.

BRAND STORY:

JIMZ is derived from the founder's name, Joshua Ityover Mishi. The brand should feel like an independent international technology company rather than a personal freelance portfolio.

DESIGN DIRECTION:

Create a premium technology/creative studio aesthetic.

The website should feel:

- sophisticated

- futuristic

- clean

- trustworthy

- innovative

- international

- creative

- commercially focused

Do NOT make it look like a generic AI SaaS template.

Do NOT overuse glowing neon effects.

Do NOT use excessive gradients.

Do NOT use childish AI imagery.

Do NOT use stock-looking robot graphics.

Use strong typography, generous whitespace, subtle motion, elegant cards, premium visual hierarchy and carefully controlled animations.

BRAND COLORS:

Deep Green: #075B3A

Orange: #FF6A00

Ivory: #FFFDF7

White: #FFFFFF

Charcoal: #111827

Muted Green: #0D7A50

Use deep green as the primary brand color.

Use orange strategically for CTAs, highlights, icons and important accents.

Use ivory and white as primary backgrounds.

Use charcoal for body text.

LOGO:

Use the JIMZ AI logo provided by the user as the primary logo asset.

Do not recreate or modify the logo.

Support light and dark background versions if logo assets are provided.
